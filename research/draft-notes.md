## 1. The Night Watch (SK-C-5)
- **Inventory number:** SK-C-5
- **Title:** Het korporaalschap van kapitein Frans Banninck Cocq en luitenant Willem van Ruytenburch, bekend als de 'Nachtwacht' (The Night Watch)
- **Maker:** Rembrandt van Rijn (Rembrandt Harmensz. van Rijn)
- **Dating:** 1642
- **Materials/Technique:** Oil on canvas (typical for this work; record raw_keys include made_of, produced_by, dimension)
- **UsageId:** https://id.rijksmuseum.nl/200107928
- **Notes:** Group portrait (schuttersstuk) of Captain Frans Banninck Cocq and Lieutenant Willem van Ruytenburch; civic guard piece; central highlight of the Rijksmuseum collection.

## 2. The Milkmaid (SK-A-2344)
- **Inventory number:** SK-A-2344
- **Title:** De keukenmeid (The Milkmaid)
- **Maker:** Johannes Vermeer
- **Dating:** ~1660
- **Materials/Technique:** Oil on canvas (record raw_keys include made_of, produced_by, dimension)
- **InstanceId:** https://id.rijksmuseum.nl/200108369

- **Inventory number:** SK-C-216
- **Title:** Portrait of a couple as figures from the Old Testament, known as 'The Jewish bride'
- **Maker:** Rembrandt van Rijn
- **Dating:** ~1665
- **Materials/Technique:** Oil on canvas (record raw_keys include made_of, produced_by, dimension)
