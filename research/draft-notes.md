## 1) The Night Watch
- Inventory number: SK-C-5
- Title: Het korporaalschap van kapitein Frans Banninck Cocq en luitenant Willem van Ruytenburch, bekend als de 'Nachtwacht'
- Maker: Rembrandt van Rijn (cross-referenced via the artist timeline: SK-C-5 appears in Rembrandt van Rijn's dated works)
- Dating: 1642
- Materials/technique: oil on canvas (record metadata includes a "made_of" entry consistent with oil on canvas)
- Link: https://id.rijksmuseum.nl/200107928

# Research trail notes
- 17th-century top-pieces search returned 762 paintings (type filter = "painting", century = 17).
- The three artworks chosen: SK-C-5 (1642, Rembrandt — flagship), SK-C-6 (1662, Rembrandt), SK-A-2344 (1660, Vermeer).
- Full records were pulled for all three via the Linked Art/details endpoint; attribution fields were cross-verified against per-artist timelines since the summary records returned blank creator fields.
